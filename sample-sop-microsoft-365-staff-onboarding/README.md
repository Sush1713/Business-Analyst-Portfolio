#  Sample SOP: Setting Up a New Staff Member on Microsoft 365

**Author:** Susmita Kumari Giri  
**Type:** Process documentation sample  
**Date:** October 2026

---

##  The Goal

By the end of their first day, a new staff member should be able to log in and do their job, with access to what their role needs, and no more than that.

This SOP gives a coordinator a clear, repeatable process to make that happen, working with the hiring manager, an outside IT partner and system owners.

---

##  At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Written for** | Business & Technology Coordinator |
| **Covers** | Microsoft 365 account, ERP and CRM access, device setup, day-one support |
| **Time needed** | About 1 to 1.5 hours, spread over the week before the start date |
| **Length** | 10 steps, 4 tables, final checklist |

---

##  The Process in Brief

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 5 days before | Collect details, get manager approval, raise a ticket with the IT partner |
| 3 days before | Check the account, add to team groups, request ERP and CRM access |
| 1 day before | Check the device, email the hiring manager |
| Day one | Share the password safely, help the person log in and set up MFA |
| End of week one | Check in, fix any gaps, close the ticket |

---

##  What This Shows About My Work

- **Process documentation:** clear steps in plain English that anyone can follow
- **Stakeholder coordination:** roles and responsibilities for each person involved
- **Security awareness:** least-access approach, MFA, safe password sharing
- **Problem solving:** a troubleshooting table with clear rules on when to escalate
- **Quality control:** a final checklist before closing the ticket
- **Thinking ahead:** a separate section for frontline and warehouse staff
